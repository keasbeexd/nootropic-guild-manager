# Nootropic Guild Manager 3.0: One guild, two places

Status: **design draft for discussion. Nothing here is built.** Items marked **[open]** need a decision; **[verify]** needs a fact checked before we rely on it.

## 1. The idea

A guild is the same group of people whether they're in WoW or in Discord. Today the two barely know about each other. NGM 3.0 makes them feel like one place:
- what you do in game shows up in Discord;
- what you do in Discord counts in game;
- the guild's activity in both feeds one picture: who's around, who's social, who's earned a promotion.

Others (Guilds of WoW, Guilds Forever) link accounts and post events. NGM goes further: **activity in and out of game counts together**, for things like auto-ranks and the Hall of Fame.

## 2. Ground rules

1. **Discord is optional.** A guild that never connects anything keeps today's addon, complete and unchanged.
2. **Works for any guild,** set up by a non-technical officer in a few minutes. Nothing is specific to <Nootropic>.
3. **Free.** Blizzard's add-on policy forbids charging for the addon or for services related to it (§10).
4. **Nothing is lost** when nobody is running the game connector. It only delays things (§5).
5. **Privacy:** we count *that* people talked, never *what* they said. Members can opt out (§11).

## 3. What members see

### In Discord
- **Roles match the game automatically:** rank, class, main or alt, level bands, "Raider". They update when someone is promoted, levels up or leaves.
- **`/profile Name`:** spec, professions, usual play hours, raid readiness, champion titles, last seen (game and Discord).
- **`/craft flask`:** who in the guild can make it, online crafters first.
- **A Hall of Fame channel** kept up to date, with champions announced on the 2nd.
- **Automatic posts:** joins, leaves, promotions, level-60 dings, first boss kills, champions. Each one optional, in a channel the guild picks.
- **Events** (Discord's own scheduled events) with sign-ups that show class, role and readiness from the game.

### In game (the addon)
- **Profiles show** the Discord name and avatar, "in Discord voice now", and last seen in Discord.
- **Play Together** includes guildmates hanging out in Discord voice.
- **An Events page:** Discord events with countdowns, a login reminder, and sign-up with your character.
- **Officers get one-click prompts:** "Rat Legend qualifies for Member: promote?"

### Shared between both
- **Social Butterfly counts guild chat in game *and* Discord** (text, and optionally voice), with the same anti-spam rules (§6).
- **Last seen = anywhere.** Active in Discord every day but rarely in game isn't "inactive".
- **Auto-ranks:** rules the guild sets, such as "Trial → Member after 14 days and 5 hours of Social Butterfly". Discord roles change automatically. In game an officer confirms with one click, because the game requires a person to click rank changes (§7).
- **Nudges:** members who haven't linked Discord, or don't have the addon, get a friendly reminder.

## 4. Where the information comes from

| Source | What it gives | Live? | Needs anyone to run anything? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blizzard's web API** (WoW: Forever) | Guild roster (rank, class, level), characters, statistics, "Sign in with Battle.net" | Minutes | No |
| **Blizzard's guild chat ↔ Discord link** (built into the game since patch 12.1, works on Forever) | Every in-game guild chat message appears in one Discord channel. For members who haven't linked their Discord it's a placeholder ("Milk Solid: sent a message to guild chat but has not yet linked their Discord account"), but **who** and **when** are still there. | Live | No |
| **Discord itself** | Text activity in all channels, voice time, events, sign-ups | Live | No |
| **The addon** | Addon-only data: recipes, play hours, profiles, raid readiness, Hall of Fame numbers, sign-ups made in game | When the connector runs | **One member runs the connector** (§5) |

**[verify]** How Blizzard's link posts look to a bot: a webhook or Blizzard's app, how the character or Discord name is attached, and how placeholders appear. Peon's read-only `--inspect-channel` reports exactly that (nootropics-guild-discord PR #8).

## 5. How it fits together

```
        Blizzard web API ──────────────┐
   (roster, characters, stats, login)  │
                                       ▼
   WoW ── Blizzard's chat link ──► NGM HUB ◄──── Discord (bot: text, voice,
    │                                  ▲            events, roles, posts)
    │        connector (a courier)     │
    └──────────────────────────────────┘
      addon-only data out; Discord data in at the next /reload
```

- **The NGM Hub** is the guild's shared memory, plus the Discord bot. It runs on our server, or self-hosted by a guild.
- **The connector is a courier, not a recorder.** The addon keeps recording everything in game, exactly as today, and its sync spreads it between players. The connector just carries that to the Hub, and carries Discord data back into the game:
  - **Any member** can run it, not only officers, after linking Discord and Battle.net. More connectors mean fresher data; one is enough.
  - **Forms:** a **browser tab** (Chrome or Edge: pick the WoW folder once, no install), or an optional small desktop app.
  - **Missing a session loses nothing.** The data waits in the addon (and in guildmates' copies) and goes up the next time anyone runs the connector. Discord data waits in the Hub.
  - **Discord → game only arrives at login or `/reload`.** Nothing can push into a running game.
- **Works with no connector at all:** roles, linking, roster posts, Discord activity, in-game chat activity (through Blizzard's link), the Hall of Fame from Blizzard's statistics.

## 6. Activity: Social Butterfly and "last seen"

The same rule everywhere. The day is cut into **15-minute blocks**. You're social in a block if you spoke (anywhere) **and** someone else did too. At most **8 blocks a day**. Spamming counts once; talking to yourself counts nothing.

- **Sources:** in-game guild chat (from Blizzard's link, plus each member's addon as a fallback), Discord text, and Discord voice (time spent in voice with at least one other member, if the guild turns it on).
- **No double counting:** the Hub merges blocks, not totals. One block seen by the bot, the addon and Discord is still one block. To make that possible, the addon will share *which* blocks it counted, not just a total.
- **Settings per guild:** which sources count, and weights (for example, voice counts half).
- **Last seen** is the latest of: in game (roster API or addon), Discord text, Discord voice.

## 7. Auto-ranks

- The guild writes rules: "Trial → Member after **14 days** in the guild and **5 hours** of Social Butterfly", or "Member → Veteran after 90 days and level 60".
- **Discord:** the role changes automatically once a rule is met.
- **In game:** the game requires a person to click rank changes; it doesn't let addons do it alone. Officers see "Rat Legend qualifies for Member" with a **Promote** button. The addon's existing secure button runs `/gpromote` on the officer's click, the same way it runs `/ginvite` for invites today.
- **Demotions** are never automatic. They can only be suggested ("inactive 60 days in game and Discord").

## 8. Hub settings (per guild)

Set from Discord (`/ngm settings`) or a small web page (log in with Discord). The basics are also changeable from the addon's Options, carried by the connector.
- **Channels:** Hall of Fame, champions, joins/leaves/promotions, dings, events.
- **Roles:** which rank maps to which role; class roles; main role; level bands.
- **Auto-rank rules,** and whether to suggest demotions.
- **Social Butterfly:** sources and weights. **Last seen:** which sources count.
- **Who may change settings:** Guild Master only, or officers.
- **Reminders:** nudge members to link Discord or install the addon (on or off).
- **Privacy:** what members can opt out of.

## 9. Setting up a new guild

1. Invite the NGM bot to the Discord server.
2. The Guild Master runs `/ngm setup` and signs in with Battle.net. That proves they lead the guild, and the Hub reads the roster.
3. Pick channels and role rules (with sensible defaults).
4. Optional: in game, link guild chat to a Discord channel with Blizzard's own setting (Guild & Communities → Guild Settings → Discord Settings). Never link a private officer channel; Blizzard warns everyone in the guild can read it.
5. Optional: members link their Discord with `/ngm link`, and anyone who wants runs the connector tab while playing.

## 10. Costs and Blizzard's policy

- **Blizzard's UI Add-On Development Policy, rule 1:** add-ons must be free, with no "premium" versions and no charging for "services related to the add-on". **Rule 5:** donation requests only on the website or download pages, never in game.
- **So:** everything free. The hosted Hub is paid for by:
  - **CurseForge author rewards,** paid on downloads;
  - **donations** (Ko-fi, GitHub Sponsors or Patreon), linked from CurseForge, Wago, GitHub, the website and the bot's `/about`;
  - **thank-yous that unlock nothing** (a supporter role in our own Discord, a thanks page).
- **Self-hosting** stays free and documented, for guilds that want their own.
- **Expected cost:** one small server for many guilds. Blizzard's API is free within its rate limits; Discord bots are free.
- **[open]** If a paid option is ever wanted, ask Blizzard first.

## 11. Privacy and safety

- The Hub stores **activity counts and timestamps**, never message text. Discord's privileged "message content" permission isn't needed.
- Members can opt out of being counted, linked, or named in posts.
- Data is deleted when a guild removes the bot, or a member unlinks (on request).
- The connector only carries what the addon already shares in game, under the same rules (who may write what). The Hub records which member uploaded what.
- Hall of Fame numbers can be checked against Blizzard's statistics API, which is stronger than today's ceilings.

## 12. Phases

1. **3.0: Hub and bot (no connector needed).** Battle.net setup and linking, roles from the roster, roster posts, Discord + in-game chat activity (Social Butterfly across both, last seen), auto-rank rules with Discord roles, settings.
2. **3.1: Connector.** Browser tab; addon-only data in Discord (`/profile`, `/craft`, Hall of Fame from the addon); Discord in game (Events page, voice presence, auto-rank prompts with Promote); sign-ups both ways.
3. **3.2: Polish.** Voice activity, dings and boss kills feed, nudges, self-hosting package, the optional desktop connector.
